Aglio e Olio

Description

Spaghetti aglio e olio is the perfect pasta dish for those nights where you either don't have enough time to make something extravagent or are just simply to tired to bother (which is completely understandable). This recipe is simple to prepare and is very inexpensive, a worthwhile investment for a dish of such bold yet simple taste and cozy aroma. Just try it once and I'll guarantee you'll have a new favorite for weekday dinners!

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound spaghetti noodles
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt & Pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup chopped Italian parsley
  • (Optional) 1 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Regiano cheese

Steps

  1. Add spaghetti into a large pot of boiling, salted water. Stir occasionally for 10-12 minutes before draining and setting aside.
  2. (Meanwhile) In a skillet, cook garlic in olive oil over medium heat for 5 minutes, or until garlic is golden brown. Remove from heat.
  3. Toss pasta with red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper
  4. Pour the garlic and olive oil over the pasta. Sprinkle with italian parsley (and cheese, if you have it). Toss until mixed.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
