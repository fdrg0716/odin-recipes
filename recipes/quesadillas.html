Chicken Quesadillas

Description

This chicken quesadilla is quick and easy to make, but will provide a satisfying and filling meal for anyone who sits down at your table. It's perfect for breakfast, lunch, snack, or even dinner if you are so inclined! Think of this recipe as a simple upgrade to the simple (yet still great) cheese quesadilla.

Ingredients:

  • 3-5 fresh tortillas
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cups of mexican style shredded cheese
  • 1 1/2 pound of chicken breast, chopped
  • 1/3 cup taco seasoning

Steps

  1. Cooking the fajita filling: In a large skillet of medium heat, heat olive oil. Then add the the chicken and taco seasoning. Add the peppers and onions. Once veggies soften, remove from heat.
  2. Making the quesadilla Place a tortilla on another skillet. Layer a half of it with cheese. Add the meat and veggie filling, then more cheese. Fold the tortilla and cook both sides.
  3. Serve and enjoy! Cut into sizeable pieces and enjoy the warm and tasty quesadilla!
