Shakshuka

Description

Shakshuka is the perfect dish made by gently poaching eggs in tomato sauce. It has a rich flavor familiar all throughout the Mediterranean, Middle East, and North Africa. It's the perfect breakfast to make. Plus, it's super easy!

Ingredients:

  1. 24 oz. canned whole tomatoes
  2. 1 cup bell pepper, diced
  3. 3/4 cup onion, diced
  4. 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  5. 1 tsp of coriander and paprika. 1/2 tsp of cumin. (red pepper flakes is optional)
  6. 4-6 eggs
  7. (Optional) sprinkle of fresh, chopped parsley and feta cheese

Steps

  1. Making the tomato sauce: In a large skillet of medium heat, heat olive oil. Then add the onion and bell peppers. Cook, stirring, for 4 minutes or until onions are golden brown. Add garlic and spices and cook for 1 more minute. Next, add the tomatoes, bring the mixture to a boil and simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. Adding the eggs: When the sauce has thickened, use a spoon to make a hole in the sauce. Crack the egg into the hole, and then cover and poach the eggs. Cook to your desired level of doneness.
  3. Serve and enjoy! Once everything is done, garnish with fresh parsley and feta cheese.
